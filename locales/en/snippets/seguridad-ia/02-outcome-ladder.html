Do not collapse every “success” into one number

1 · Bypass

Conversational/content policy crossed.

2 · Capability

Output materially useful for the defined threat.

3 · Tool reachability

Equivalent external action becomes proposal/reachable.

4 · Execution

Independent authorization permits a real state change.

Why this ladder matters

Different controls stop different transitions. Measuring them separately reveals whether the model, tool contract or authorization boundary is actually carrying security.
