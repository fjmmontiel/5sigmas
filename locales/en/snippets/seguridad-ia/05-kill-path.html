The stop mechanism must live outside the model

Circuit break

Stop a user, tenant, workflow or tool independently.

Revoke

Remove credentials/scopes immediately.

Cancel

Terminate in-flight work and enforce retry budgets.

Recover

Rollback or reconcile partial effects; degrade to read-only if needed.

Why natural-language “stop” is insufficient

If the model's reasoning channel is compromised, the same channel cannot be the only authority capable of terminating the workflow.
