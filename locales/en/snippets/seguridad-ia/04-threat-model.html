Threat model before benchmark

Asset

What must remain protected?

Actor

What can the adversary control or observe?

Entry

Chat, document, memory, tool output, MCP or agent?

Budget

Attempts, time, feedback and adaptivity?

Authority

Which tools/scopes can be reached?

Outcome

What event counts as harm and what recovery is required?

Why this comes first

A benchmark score is useful only when the evaluated environment matches the asset, actor, permissions and success condition you actually care about.
