Separate components by trust and authority

Quarantined reader

Can inspect untrusted content but has no sensitive tools.

Validated handoff

Structured output narrows what crosses the boundary.

Privileged decision

Sees only the context needed to decide.

Runtime policy

Authorizes user × resource × operation independently.

Why this is stronger than one system prompt

One model mistake no longer has a direct path from arbitrary text to irreversible action. Each boundary can fail independently and be tested independently.
