Space changes the constraint stack

Solar availability ↑

Favourable orbits can keep arrays illuminated far more continuously than terrestrial sites.

Cooling is not free

Vacuum removes evaporative water use but forces heat through large radiators.

Data gravity changes

Satellite-native data can be processed before downlink, reducing scarce bandwidth.

The correct comparison

Ask whether orbital energy and data locality outweigh launch mass, thermal area, networking and replacement costs for the specific workload.
