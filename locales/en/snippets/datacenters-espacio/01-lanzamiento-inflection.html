Launch cost moved from impossible to workload-dependent

Shuttle era

~$88k/kg to LEO made mass-heavy compute economically prohibitive.

Reusable launch today

Commonly cited ranges are roughly $1.4k–$2.5k/kg depending on mission.

Starship targets

Sub-$200/kg and lower scenarios are projections, not demonstrated market prices.

What actually changes at lower $/kg

Solar arrays, radiators, shielding and replacement launches all become cheaper simultaneously. That is why launch economics are the main nonlinear variable in orbital data-center models.
