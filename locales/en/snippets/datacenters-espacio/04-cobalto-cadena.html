Cobalt shows why “cloud” infrastructure has a physical geography

Mining concentration

Roughly three quarters of global mine output comes from the DRC.

Refining concentration

A large majority of refining capacity is concentrated in China.

Local impact

Mining regions can bear health, water and labour costs invisible to end users.

Responsible-sourcing implication

Traceability, worker protection, remediation and recycling are infrastructure controls—not peripheral ESG labels—when material demand scales rapidly.
