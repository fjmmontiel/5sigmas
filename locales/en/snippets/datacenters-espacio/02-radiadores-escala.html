2 MW of compute can imply ~4,000 m² of radiator

Waste heat

≈2,000,000 W must leave the spacecraft continuously.

Representative flux

≈500 W/m² at electronics-compatible radiator temperatures.

Area

≈4,000 m² before redundancy, structure or deployment margins.

Why this dominates design

Radiator area adds launch mass, deployment mechanisms, orientation constraints and debris exposure. Thermal scaling is not a minor subsystem.
