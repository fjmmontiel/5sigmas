AI infrastructure hits six terrestrial constraints at once

Grid

Generation, transmission and substations can take years.

Water

Cooling pressure matters most in stressed local basins.

Land

Large campuses need power + fibre + suitable sites.

Permits

Buildings and grid upgrades move on different timelines.

Heat

40–120 kW racks push cooling toward liquid systems.

Latency

Geographic distribution reduces delay but adds complexity.

Why this creates an orbital question

Space does not remove every constraint; it changes which resource is scarce. The comparison becomes meaningful only when a workload benefits from that change.
