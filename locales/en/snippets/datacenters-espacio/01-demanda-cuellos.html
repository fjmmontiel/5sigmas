Why terrestrial data centers are getting harder to scale

AI demand does not hit one limit. It pushes several infrastructure constraints at the same time.

Grid connection

Large new loads compete for generation, transmission and substations.

Cooling and water

Higher rack density raises heat-removal requirements and can increase local water pressure.

Land

Large campuses need space near power and fiber, often in already-constrained regions.

Permitting

The building, grid connection and associated infrastructure move on different approval timelines.

Heat

Dense accelerator racks increasingly require liquid cooling and create large heat-rejection loads.

Latency + redundancy

Distributing compute closer to users reduces latency but increases operational complexity.
