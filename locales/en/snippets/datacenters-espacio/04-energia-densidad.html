AI changes the physical density of a data center

Traditional rack

~5–15 kW.

Dense GPU rack

~50–100 kW became common in AI deployments.

GB200 NVL72 class

~120 kW at full load: liquid cooling becomes practical necessity.

Why old buildings cannot simply absorb it

Power distribution, piping, heat exchangers, floor loading and service architecture all change when rack density rises by an order of magnitude.
