Orbit turns maintenance into a reliability budget

Radiation

Bit errors, latch-ups and cumulative degradation affect commercial electronics.

Protection

Rad-hard hardware or COTS + shielding/ECC/software each trade compute for resilience.

Servicing

Redundancy and remote recovery replace ordinary technician access.

Lifecycle consequence

The hardware generation is largely frozen at launch, so obsolescence and fault tolerance must be priced into the mission rather than handled by routine rack replacement.
