A data center is a global materials supply chain

Compute

Silicon, copper and cobalt inside advanced semiconductor/interconnect systems.

Power

Lithium, nickel, manganese and cobalt in modern UPS batteries.

Networking + storage

Tantalum and rare-earth materials support capacitors, motors and optical systems.

Why this matters to AI infrastructure

Refining and mining are geographically concentrated, so scaling accelerators increases geopolitical and environmental dependencies far beyond the data-center site.
