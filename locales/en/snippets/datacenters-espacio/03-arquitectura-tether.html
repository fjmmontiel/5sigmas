A hyperscale orbital cloud needs every subsystem to scale together

Launch

High cadence + low $/kg for compute, structure and replacement mass.

Power

Kilometre-scale solar and electrical distribution.

Thermal

Kilometre-scale radiator capacity at megawatt/gigawatt heat loads.

Network

Optical mesh + ground gateways with useful latency and capacity.

Operations

Autonomous fault management, servicing and deorbit.

Why one solved subsystem is not enough

The economics are multiplicative: cheap launch cannot compensate for an unsolved thermal architecture, and abundant solar power has little value if output cannot reach its users.
