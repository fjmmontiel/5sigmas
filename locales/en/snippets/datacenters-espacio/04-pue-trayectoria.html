PUE measures facility overhead, not total compute demand

PUE 2.0

1 W of overhead for each 1 W delivered to IT.

PUE 1.1

~0.1 W of overhead for each 1 W delivered to IT.

AI era

IT watts can grow rapidly even while the overhead ratio improves.

Why PUE alone is incomplete

A highly efficient 500 MW facility can consume far more electricity than an inefficient 20 MW facility. PUE is a ratio, not a total-footprint metric.
