Economic end-of-life arrives before technical end-of-life

Frontier cluster

Latest accelerators justify premium power and floor space.

Second life

Older GPUs can remain valuable for inference, research and lower-intensity workloads.

Material recovery

Components and metals retain value after useful compute life ends.

Circular-economy signal

Large operators increasingly refurbish and redeploy server components; the relevant metric is not only e-waste generated but how much equipment is reused before recycling.
