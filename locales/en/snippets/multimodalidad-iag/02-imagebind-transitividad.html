ANCHOR ALIGNMENT

One anchor can connect modalities that were never paired directly

Image↔ text↔ audio↔ depth↔ thermal↔ IMU
The transitive idea

If text and audio are each aligned with corresponding images, their representations can become useful to one another without requiring a complete audio–text dataset for every concept.

Why image is a useful anchor

Images naturally co-occur with many other modalities on the web and in sensors, making them a practical bridge across otherwise sparse pairwise datasets.
