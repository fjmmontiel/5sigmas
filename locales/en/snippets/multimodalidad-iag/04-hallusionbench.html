VISUAL HALLUCINATION

Different wrong answers reveal different weaknesses

Existence

Claims an absent object is present.

Counting

Invents or misses instances.

Spatial

Reverses left/right, above/below or containment.

Attribute

Assigns the wrong colour, state or property.

Why one aggregate score is dangerous

A model can improve on object existence while remaining weak on spatial reasoning. Slice-level evaluation is needed to identify which failures matter for the application.
