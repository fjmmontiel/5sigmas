Risk grows when perception can change state

The safer architecture keeps interpretation, authorization and execution as separate trust boundaries.

1Perceive

Images, audio, documents and retrieved media are untrusted evidence.

2Interpret

The model may extract meaning or propose an action, but it does not grant permission.

3Verify

Policy, provenance, confidence and application-specific constraints are checked independently.

4Authorize

Only allowed, bounded operations cross into the tool or external system.

5Observe

Logs preserve the perception → reasoning → action chain for diagnosis and rollback.

6Review

Bias, privacy and regulatory requirements are evaluated on real deployment slices.
