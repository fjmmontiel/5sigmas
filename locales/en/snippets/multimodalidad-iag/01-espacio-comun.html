What alignment buys you

Cross-modal retrieval becomes a nearest-neighbour problem: a text query can retrieve an image or audio clip because semantically related inputs occupy nearby regions.

What it does not guarantee

A shared embedding space is excellent for similarity and retrieval, but it does not by itself provide fine-grained generation, long-horizon reasoning or action.
