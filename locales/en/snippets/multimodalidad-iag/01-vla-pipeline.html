PERCEPTION → ACTION

Multimodality can end in an action, not text

A vision-language-action system keeps perceptual evidence connected to instructions until it produces an executable control decision.

Image / state+Instruction→Multimodal representation→Plan→Action tokens
Why this changes the risk model

A perceptual error can propagate into planning and then into the environment. Grounding, uncertainty and action validation matter more than they do for a text-only answer.
