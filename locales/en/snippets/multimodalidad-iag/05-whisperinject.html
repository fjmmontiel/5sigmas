AUDIO AS UNTRUSTED INPUT

Meaning extracted from sound must not become authorization

Audio stream→Audio-language model→Interpreted request→ policy check →Allowed action
Why native audio changes security

The system may interpret speech, background sounds and other acoustic content continuously. Application permissions and confirmations must remain independent of what the model hears.

Useful controls

Explicit tool scopes, typed arguments, provenance, confirmation for consequential actions and logging of the audio-to-action chain.
