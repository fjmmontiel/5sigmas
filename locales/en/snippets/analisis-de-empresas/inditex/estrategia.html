Two readings that can coexist
Our interpretationCoordination and distribution

AI could strengthen coordinated decisions in an integrated commercial system and open new ways of distributing the offer.

In the passages reviewed, we do not identify an attributable operational impact.

Our interpretationDefensive diffusion of tools

Inditex could be spreading AI tools internally to defend efficiency and service quality, without a unified coordination strategy that can be demonstrated from outside.

The scarcity of detail fits broad undisclosed adoption or limited experiments.

Evidence we would look for, valid for both readings
  • The prior process and a consistent comparison of availability, error and cost
  • Documented scope and human intervention
  • A breakdown of AI investment and full costs
  • Retention, oversight and recourse in the affected markets and systems

Lack of publication, by itself, does not prove they do not exist.

For a measured use, an improvement that disappears against a comparable group, or full costs that exceed the benefit, would weaken its value.

Our own horizon, conditional. No signal is guaranteed in the next 12–24 months and we assign no probabilities.
