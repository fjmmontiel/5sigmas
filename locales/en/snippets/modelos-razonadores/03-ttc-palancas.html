Test-time compute

Three levers for spending more inference compute

More compute can mean a longer trajectory, more independent trajectories, or a more structured search. Each buys quality with a different cost and latency profile.

Chain-of-thought

The model produces intermediate reasoning before the final answer. Longer chains help when later decisions depend on earlier subproblems.

Prompt→Step 1→Step 2→Step N→Answer
Budget forcingA stopping token can be suppressed and a continuation cue such as “Wait” can force additional deliberation. This can recover some prematurely wrong answers, but it also adds tokens and latency.
Best fit chained mathematics, multi-step logic, complex codeCost shape roughly proportional to additional generated reasoning tokens
Best-of-N · self-consistency

Generate N independent responses, then select by a verifier, reward model, majority vote or model-as-judge.

Statistical advantageIf candidates are sufficiently diverse, increasing N raises the chance that at least one trajectory solves the problem correctly. Selection quality becomes the next bottleneck.
Best fit verifiable mathematics and executable codeCost shape approximately N × the cost of one candidate
