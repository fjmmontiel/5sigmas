Overthinking: when more reasoning makes the answer worse
Reasoning has a task-dependent optimum. Past that point, extra exploration can add doubt and error instead of quality.

Apple Research documented the qualitative pattern: reasoning can improve early, reach a useful region and then degrade if the system keeps reopening already-solved questions.

The exact optimum varies by task and model. The reusable design insight is that marginal reasoning value can become negative.

1 · Analyze

The model identifies the relevant structure and reaches a coherent solution.

↓
2 · Verify

The solution survives a useful check. A bounded system could stop here.

↓
3 · Re-open

Without new evidence, the model starts exploring increasingly unlikely alternatives.

↓
4 · Degrade

The final answer becomes less precise or less correct than the earlier verified result.

Adaptive budgets

Estimate problem complexity and allocate less reasoning to simple tasks, more to genuinely hard ones.

Early stopping

Stop when independent checks converge or additional steps stop reducing uncertainty.

Bounded verification

Verify the current answer without repeatedly reopening already-settled premises.

The objective is not to minimize reasoning. It is to stop paying for reasoning once its expected marginal value turns negative.
