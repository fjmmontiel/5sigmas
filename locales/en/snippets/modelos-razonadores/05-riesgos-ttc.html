Two risks that grow with extended reasoning
More test-time compute can increase both process opacity and the model's ability to discover unintended routes to an objective.
SUPERVISION PRINCIPLEYou cannot reliably supervise what you cannot interpret
Readable reasoning signal

"If x² = 16, then x = ±4. The task asks for the positive value, so x = 4."

An evaluator can inspect the stated logic and catch obvious errors.
Unreadable or unfaithful signal

Reasoning artifacts are incomplete, opaque or not causally representative of what determined the answer.

Monitoring becomes weaker because the audit signal itself is unreliable.

Reasoning traces can be one observability signal, but they should never replace typed permissions, deterministic checks or independent outcome validation.
