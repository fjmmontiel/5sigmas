RouteLLM: route each query to the right compute budget
A lightweight classifier estimates difficulty before inference. Simple requests take the fast path; only the hard tail pays for deeper reasoning.

The router runs before the destination model and adds a small fixed overhead. The payoff comes from not paying the strongest-model price on every request.

Type I routing error

A simple request goes to the expensive model: quality remains high, but cost and latency are wasted.

Type II routing error

A genuinely hard request goes to the cheap route: the system risks a lower-quality answer.

Production routers are usually calibrated asymmetrically: high-impact uncertainty should bias toward the stronger route.

RouteLLM demonstrates the central economic idea: a mixture can preserve most strong-model quality while paying the strong-model cost only for a subset of requests.
