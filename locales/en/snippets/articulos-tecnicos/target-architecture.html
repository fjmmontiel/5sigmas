Three planes, one durable protocol

Interaction surface

Turns, acknowledgements, playback and pending delivery.

Durable coordination

Operation truth, idempotency, ordering, cancellation and recovery.

Cognitive execution

Reasoning, RAG, tools, workers, retries and side effects.

Ownership rule

The worker owns work completion; the interaction surface owns human-visible delivery; the coordination layer preserves the relationship across retries and restarts.
