Hybrid architecture: fast S2S surface, heavier cognitive plane

S2S interaction surface

Listen, backchannel, manage barge-in, answer lightweight turns and deliver pending work.

Durable event contract

Operations and deliveries cross the boundary with explicit IDs/state.

Cognitive execution plane

Deep reasoning, RAG, tools, parallel workers, validation and side effects.

Why this split is attractive

Human timing can stay fast and full duplex while slow business work runs independently and returns through the same proactive-delivery protocol.
