MLOPS

A trained model is only one stage

1 Data→2 Prepare→3 Train→4 Evaluate→5 Version→6 Deploy→7 Monitor→8 Feedback↺
Why the loop matters

Production data changes. A system therefore needs traceable model/data versions, pre-deployment evaluation, operational monitoring and a feedback path for retraining or rollback.

Model quality vs system quality

A strong offline metric cannot compensate for broken data pipelines, serving latency, silent schema changes or unmonitored drift. MLOps treats those as part of the ML product itself.
