THE AI STACK

Four labels, different scopes

Each layer narrows the family of systems being described.

Artificial IntelligenceAny engineered system performing tasks associated with intelligent behaviour.
Machine LearningAI whose behaviour is learned from data rather than only explicit rules.
Deep LearningML based on multi-layer neural networks.
Generative AIModels whose primary output is newly generated content.
Do the sets always nest perfectly?

This is a practical teaching hierarchy, not a complete taxonomy. Generative modelling predates deep learning, and modern products often combine several families. The key distinction is what technology learns and what the system outputs.
