What training changes

Backpropagation computes how the loss changes with each weight and bias; an optimizer then updates those parameters. Deep networks repeat this across many layers and millions or billions of parameters.

Why activations matter

Without nonlinear activation functions, stacking layers would collapse into one linear transformation. Nonlinearity lets a network represent interactions, curves and conditional structure.
