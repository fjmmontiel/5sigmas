K-MEANS

Move prototypes until groups become compact

●●●A●●●B
  1. Choose k initial centers.
  2. Assign each point to its nearest center.
  3. Move each center to the mean of its assigned points.
  4. Repeat until assignments stabilize.
What training changes

The learned parameters are the cluster-center coordinates. Distance metric and feature scaling define what “similar” means, so preprocessing is part of the model behaviour.

What it does not know

K-means receives no semantic labels. It can discover groups, but a human or downstream process still has to interpret whether those groups correspond to useful customer segments or other real concepts.
