NAIVE BAYES

Count evidence, then combine probabilities

P(spam | words) ∝ P(spam) × Π P(word | spam)
What training changes

Training updates class priors and feature-frequency tables. The “naive” assumption treats observed signals as conditionally independent given the class, which makes inference simple and fast.

Why it is still useful

For bag-of-words text and small datasets, Naive Bayes can be extremely fast, data-efficient and interpretable even though its independence assumption is rarely literally true.
