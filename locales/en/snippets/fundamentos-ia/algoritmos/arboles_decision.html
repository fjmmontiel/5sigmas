What training changes

The algorithm searches candidate features and thresholds, chooses splits that improve class separation, and grows/prunes the tree structure. Random Forest and boosting methods combine many such trees.

Why trees dominate many tabular problems

They handle mixed numeric/categorical signals, nonlinear interactions and missingness well, often with less preprocessing than neural networks.
