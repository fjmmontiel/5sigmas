What training adds

The initial vectors are just coordinates. Training moves them until geometry becomes useful for the prediction objective, so concepts used in related contexts develop related representations.

Why this matters beyond text

Images, audio and other modalities can also be mapped into learned vector spaces, making cross-modal comparison and shared architectures possible.
