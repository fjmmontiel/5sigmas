IF CAPABILITY BECOMES GENERAL

Three consequences become coupled

Economic

Broad automation could affect analysis, design, research and decision work, not only repetitive tasks.

Scientific

Systems that read literature, propose hypotheses and design experiments could compress discovery cycles.

Alignment

The more capable the system, the more important it becomes that its objective and constraints match intended human outcomes.

The alignment problem in one sentence

A powerful optimizer can be dangerous without malicious intent if the objective it actually optimizes is an imperfect proxy for what people meant.

Why this is technically difficult

Human preferences are incomplete, contextual and sometimes conflicting. A specification that works in familiar cases can fail under distribution shift or when a system discovers strategies its designers did not anticipate.
