MEASURING PROGRESS

No single benchmark answers “is this AGI?”

Coding

Measures software task completion under a benchmark environment.

Reasoning

ARC-style tasks test adaptation to unfamiliar abstract problems.

Task horizon

METR measures how long a sustained task a model can complete at a chosen reliability.

Science / olympiad

Tests high-end problem solving in bounded formal domains.

Why scores can become stale

Benchmarks can saturate, leak into training data or stop representing frontier difficulty. Strong evaluation therefore needs refreshed tasks, contamination analysis and multiple independent dimensions.

What matters for autonomy

A model that answers hard questions can still fail on long sequences of actions. Task duration, recovery from mistakes and tool-use reliability are distinct from isolated accuracy.
