Why parameter count is not the whole story

Data quality and quantity, training compute, architecture, optimization and post-training all matter. Parameter count is useful historically because it makes the scale jump tangible, not because bigger is automatically better.

Capability thresholds

As models became stronger, tasks such as in-context/few-shot adaptation became practically useful. Whether those changes are mathematically discontinuous “emergence” or smooth improvements exposed by thresholded metrics remains debated.
