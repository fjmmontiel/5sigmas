CAPABILITY ≠ GENERALITY

Strong in many domains, still uneven

Strong today
  • Language and coding
  • Large-context analysis
  • Tool/computer use
  • Selected math/science benchmarks
Still unreliable
  • Robust causal reasoning
  • Persistent learning
  • Out-of-distribution transfer
  • Reliable uncertainty awareness
  • Long-horizon autonomy
Why benchmark strength is not AGI

Benchmarks sample particular capabilities under particular conditions. General intelligence requires robust transfer across unfamiliar tasks, time horizons and environments—not only high average scores on known evaluations.

Grounding matters

Language and multimodal models learn rich representations of the world, but reliable physical interaction adds dynamics, perception, control and consequences that text-only competence does not automatically solve.
