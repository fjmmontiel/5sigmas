AGI LEVELS

Performance and generality are separate axes

0No AI1Emerging2Competent3Expert4Virtuoso5Superintelligence
Why a level is not one benchmark score

A system can be superhuman in chess, protein prediction or one coding benchmark while remaining weak outside that domain. AGI frameworks therefore need both breadth and performance.

Different definitions move the threshold

Cognitive, economic and safety-oriented definitions measure different things: broad human-like competence, economically valuable autonomy, or capabilities that could accelerate further AI development.
