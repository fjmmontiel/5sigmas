These bars are conceptual, not benchmark scores

The purpose is to show profile shape: modern AI can exceed expert humans in selected bounded domains while remaining less robust on embodied, persistent and unfamiliar long-horizon tasks.

What generality would require

Broad capability must survive domain changes. The harder test is not one superhuman peak but reliable transfer, uncertainty handling, memory, tool use and recovery across many kinds of work.
