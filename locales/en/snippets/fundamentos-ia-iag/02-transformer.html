Self-attention

Each position constructs queries, keys and values and computes weighted combinations of information from other positions. Different attention heads can learn different relationships.

Why it scaled

Unlike recurrent processing, Transformer training can process many sequence positions in parallel. That made efficient accelerator utilization and very large pretraining runs substantially easier.
