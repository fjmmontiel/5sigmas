CLASSICAL ML ↔ GENERATIVE AI

Five differences that change engineering decisions

Outputfinite label / score↔open-ended generation

Reproducibilityusually stable inference↔probabilistic decoding

Explainabilityoften feature-level↔internal reasoning opaque

Evaluationground-truth metrics↔multi-criteria evals

Riskdrift/adversarial inputs↔hallucination/injection/tool misuse

Why neither side is universally better

The correct architecture follows the task. A low-latency binary classifier and an open-ended document assistant optimize different objectives and should not be forced into the same technology stack.
