WORLD MODELS

Simulate before acting

A world model tries to represent how an environment evolves so an agent can evaluate possible future trajectories before committing to an action.

One-step sequence model
stateₜ → predict stateₜ₊₁

Continue the sequence one transition at a time.

World-model planning
stateₜ → {future A, B, C} → score → act

Imagine alternative futures, reject poor outcomes and choose a trajectory.

Why anticipation matters
  • Prevention: reject actions whose simulated consequences are undesirable.
  • Planning: compare multi-step trajectories rather than optimizing one immediate prediction.
  • Physical agents: robotics and control require reasoning about dynamics before acting.
Representative research directions

DreamerV3 learns a latent dynamics model and plans/learns inside imagined rollouts. I-JEPA predicts abstract representations rather than reconstructing every pixel. Genie 2 explores large-scale generative interactive environments.

Open question

How much world structure can be learned implicitly by large sequence models, and when does explicit latent dynamics modeling provide a decisive advantage? The answer remains domain-dependent and actively researched.
