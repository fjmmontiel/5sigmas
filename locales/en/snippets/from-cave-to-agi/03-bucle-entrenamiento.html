TRAINING LOOP

Three pieces, one mechanism

From small statistical models to modern neural networks, learning means measuring error, assigning responsibility for it, and updating parameters.

Data→Predict→Loss→Backprop→Update↺
1 · Measure the error

A loss function turns the mismatch between prediction and target into a scalar objective. Without a measure of error there is no direction for improvement.

ℒ = − Σ y · log(ŷ)
y = target · ŷ = prediction · ℒ = loss
2 · Backpropagate responsibility

The chain rule propagates the output error backward through the network and estimates how much each parameter contributed.

∂ℒ/∂W = ∂ℒ/∂a · ∂a/∂W
Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams (1986) made this the standard recipe for multilayer networks.
3 · Update the parameters

Stochastic gradient descent moves each weight a small step opposite the gradient. Repeating this over batches progressively reduces the objective.

W ← W − η · ∇ℒ(W)
η = learning rate · ∇ℒ(W) = gradient
