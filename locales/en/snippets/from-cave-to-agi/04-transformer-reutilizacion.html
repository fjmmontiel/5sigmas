TRANSFORMER · 2017+

Attention unlocked reuse

The Transformer changed more than accuracy: it made large-scale parallel pretraining practical and helped turn one general model into a reusable base for many downstream tasks.

Before: sequential recurrence
t₁→t₂→t₃→t₄

RNNs and LSTMs process sequence states step by step. Training is difficult to parallelize across time and long dependencies must travel through intermediate states.

Transformer: direct attention
t₁t₂t₃t₄

Self-attention lets each position directly combine information from other positions. The computation can be parallelized far more effectively during training.

The reusable-model loop
  1. Pretrain at scale on broad corpora.
  2. Learn transferable representations rather than features for one task only.
  3. Adapt downstream through prompting, fine-tuning or other specialization.
One base→ translation→ summarization→ code→ domain assistants

The key shift was technical and economic: expensive general learning could be amortized across many applications.
