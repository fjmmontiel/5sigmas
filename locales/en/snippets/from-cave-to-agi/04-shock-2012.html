ALEXNET · 2012

2012 changed the regime

The jump was not a small benchmark gain. Data scale, GPU compute and deep learned representations converged strongly enough to redirect computer vision.

The jump in ImageNet top-5 error

Lower is better. AlexNet cut roughly ten percentage points at once after years of much smaller changes.

Why it worked
1.2M images

ImageNet provided a training dataset at unprecedented scale.

2 GPUs

GTX 580s made the matrix-heavy training workload practical.

Deep CNN

ReLU, dropout, convolution and learned hierarchical features turned raw pixels into useful representations.

The consequence
Before

Human-designed features such as SIFT/HOG, then a task-specific classifier.

After

Networks increasingly learned useful features directly from data.

The lesson was not “architecture no longer matters.” It was that good architectures can remain underpowered until data and compute stop being the bottleneck.
