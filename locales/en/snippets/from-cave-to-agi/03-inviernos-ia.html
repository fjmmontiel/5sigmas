AI WINTERS · 1956–2012

The same boom-and-bust pattern, twice

Two cycles shared the same failure mode: expectations outran what the technology and institutions of the time could actually deliver.

1956–1974 · First summer → first winter
SummerDartmouth · perceptron · general-AI forecasts→WinterALPAC · Minsky & Papert · Lighthill · funding cuts

Pattern: oversized promise → visible limits → reduced funding.

1980–1993 · Expert systems → second winter
SummerMYCIN · XCON · corporate investment→Winterrule maintenance fails to scale · Lisp-machine collapse · program cuts

The mechanism was similar even though the technology was different.

1993–2012 · What changed quietly
Data

Internet-scale digitization and ImageNet created unprecedented training corpora.

GPUs

Commodity graphics hardware matched the matrix operations used by neural training.

Benchmarks

Shared challenges made improvements measurable, comparable and cumulative.

2012: AlexNet made the convergence impossible to ignore.
