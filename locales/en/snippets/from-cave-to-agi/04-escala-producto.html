Large pretrained models demonstrated broad capability; alignment and product design made those capabilities usable through a public conversational interface.

The technical gap: alignment
Base model

→ instruction tuning / preference learning →
Assistant

Alignment changes the objective from “what text is likely next?” toward “what response is useful and preferred under these instructions?”

ChatGPT: capability becomes an interface

The product breakthrough was not only more compute. A conversational UI, aligned behavior and low-friction access let non-specialists interact directly with a general-purpose language model.

Scaling became something users could touch.
