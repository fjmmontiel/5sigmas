2025–2026 CAPITAL SIGNALS

The frontier is scaling beyond the model

Investment is spreading across foundation models, compute infrastructure, dependable energy and physical-world systems. The implicit thesis is that the next frontier needs an end-to-end stack.

Infrastructure becomes strategic

Cloud capacity, accelerators and data centers determine how much model training and inference can actually be delivered. Compute availability is now a product constraint, not only a research input.

Energy and physical deployment become bottlenecks

Power agreements and new nuclear projects respond to the electricity intensity of AI infrastructure, while robotics and spatial-intelligence investment reflects a parallel push to give models perception and action beyond the screen.

Interpretation: capital does not prove which technical path will win. It does reveal where the ecosystem expects constraints to bind: models, compute, energy and the physical world.
