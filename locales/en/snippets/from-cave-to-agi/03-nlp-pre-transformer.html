NLP BEFORE TRANSFORMERS

Three approaches, one recurring limitation

Statistical NLP made real progress for decades, but context stayed local and useful representations were largely engineered by humans.

N-grams · local next-word statistics

A trigram estimates the next word from only the previous two words. For the cat eats ___, local counts can rank likely continuations—but information nine words back is invisible.

HMMs · hidden states generate observations

Hidden Markov Models treat grammatical categories as latent states and use algorithms such as Viterbi to infer the most likely sequence.

The Markov assumption keeps the model tractable, but long-range semantic dependencies remain difficult.

CRFs · richer features, still hand-designed

Conditional Random Fields directly model label sequences conditioned on observed words and can use arbitrary features. They became strong tools for tasks such as named-entity recognition.
