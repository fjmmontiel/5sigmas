SYMBOLIC AI · 1950s–1980s

Intelligence as a list of rules

Expert systems encoded human knowledge explicitly. They were strong inside bounded domains and fragile when the rule base had to absorb an open world.

Expert→Rules→Inference engine→Decision
Why it worked

Every decision could be audited: the system could identify exactly which rule fired and why. That is valuable when the vocabulary and operating conditions are stable.

MYCIN: a real expert system

MYCIN used roughly 450 hand-authored rules to recommend antibiotics for serious bacterial infections. Rules carried certainty factors, for example: IF organism is gram-positive AND site is blood → consider Staphylococcus (0.7).

The scaling limit

The bottleneck was human as much as computational: extracting, formalizing, reconciling and maintaining expert knowledge became slow and brittle.
