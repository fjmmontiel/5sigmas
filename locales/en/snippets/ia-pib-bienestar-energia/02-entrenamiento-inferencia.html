Training and inference create different infrastructure loads

Training is a concentrated project workload. Inference becomes a persistent service workload whose total demand follows product usage.

Training
Build the model
  • Large accelerator clusters
  • High utilization over a bounded run
  • Throughput matters more than per-user latency
  • Electricity concentrated before deployment
Inference
Serve the model
  • Requests arrive continuously
  • Latency and availability are product constraints
  • Capacity must follow traffic peaks
  • Demand compounds with adoption
one model-building project→many recurring user/application requests
For a widely used model, lifetime serving demand can become more important than the electricity consumed by a single training run.
