AI electricity is only one part of the hardware footprint

Accelerators also require an industrial supply chain: semiconductor fabrication, packaging, memory, networking equipment and data-center construction all consume materials and energy before the first inference request runs.

Silicon + materials→Wafer fabrication→Advanced packaging→Accelerator + memory→Data center
Fabrication plants

Leading-edge fabs use large amounts of electricity, ultrapure water and process equipment.

Packaging + memory

Modern AI systems depend on tightly integrated high-bandwidth memory and advanced packaging, not only the compute die.

Network + cooling

Thousands of accelerators need switching, power conversion and thermal systems around them.

Embodied footprint

The energy used to manufacture and build infrastructure is separate from the electricity consumed during operation.

Operational electricity is usually the dominant metric in data-center discussions, but a full lifecycle analysis also counts the hardware and infrastructure created to deliver that compute.
