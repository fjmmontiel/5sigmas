Compute becomes electricity through an entire power-delivery stack

The accelerator is only the final load. Grid power is transformed, conditioned and distributed through data-center electrical systems before it reaches the chips.

Grid / onsite generation→Substation→Transformers + UPS→Power distribution→Servers→Accelerators
IT load

The electricity consumed by servers, accelerators, memory, storage and networking.

Cooling

Heat removed from the IT equipment becomes an additional facility electricity requirement.

Power conversion

Transformers, UPS systems and voltage conversion introduce losses between the grid and the chips.

PUE

Power Usage Effectiveness compares total facility electricity with the electricity used by IT equipment.

facility electricity≈IT electricity×PUE
A more efficient facility reduces overhead around the compute load, but it does not remove the electricity consumed by the workload itself.
