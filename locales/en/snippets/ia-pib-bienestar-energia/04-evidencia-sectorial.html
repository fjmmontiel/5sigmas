Do not copy a task gain directly into a GDP forecast

Task

Time, quality or completion rate.

Team

Review and coordination still matter.

Firm

Saved time must become durable output.

Sector

Adoption must diffuse across firms.

Economy

Prices, demand and reallocation shape GDP.

How to read 20–50% task gains

They are strong upstream evidence in the measured workflow, not a claim that firm productivity or national output rises by the same percentage.
