Different AI modalities create different compute profiles

Text, image, audio and video all become numerical workloads, but the amount of data processed and generated per request can differ by orders of magnitude.

Text

Usually the lightest modality per unit of output, though long context and long responses increase work.

Audio

Continuous signals create many time steps and often require real-time serving constraints.

Images

Generation operates over large latent or pixel representations rather than a short token sequence.

Video

Many frames plus temporal consistency make generation substantially more compute-intensive.

Illustrative, not a universal ranking.Actual energy depends on model architecture, hardware, resolution, context length, batch size and serving implementation.
