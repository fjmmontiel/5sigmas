GDP and well-being answer different questions

Economic activity is measurable and important, but it is not a complete model of how people are doing.

Market production

Goods and services exchanged in the economy.

Income and output

Scale and growth of measured economic activity.

Comparability

A standardized framework across time and countries.

Distribution

Averages can hide who receives the gains.

Unpaid work

Care, household production and volunteering often sit outside markets.

Non-market outcomes

Health, security, environment, trust and life satisfaction need separate measures.

Activity

Use GDP for the size and direction of market production.

Outcomes

Add health, education, distribution and environmental measures.

Experience

Add subjective well-being when the question is how people perceive their lives.
