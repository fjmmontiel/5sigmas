Different indicators answer different questions

GDP

How much market output is produced?

HDI

What are the population's health, education and income capabilities?

GPI

What changes after adding unpaid work and subtracting social or environmental costs?

Better Life

How do housing, jobs, safety, environment, community and life satisfaction compare?

Best practice

Use a measurement stack rather than searching for one perfect replacement: production, distribution, quality/access and lived outcomes belong in separate columns.
