Forecasts disagree because their assumptions disagree

Broader transformation

More tasks affected, faster diffusion, strong complementary investment and creation of new demand → larger output scenarios.

Narrower transformation

Fewer automatable tasks, slower adoption and limited new-task creation → much smaller productivity effects.

Five questions to ask before trusting a headline number
  1. Which tasks are assumed transformable?
  2. How fast does adoption spread?
  3. How much workflow redesign is assumed?
  4. Are new tasks and demand included?
  5. What is the time horizon?
