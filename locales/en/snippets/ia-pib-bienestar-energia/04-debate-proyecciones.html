AI macro projections disagree because their assumptions differ

The headline number is less informative than the model behind it: task exposure, adoption, complementarity and creation of new work.

Higher-impact scenariosBroad adoption + strong complementarity

Assume AI reaches many tasks, firms reorganize successfully and new demand expands.

Lower-impact scenariosNarrow task coverage + limited reorganization

Assume automation concentrates on a smaller set of tasks and diffusion remains incomplete.
