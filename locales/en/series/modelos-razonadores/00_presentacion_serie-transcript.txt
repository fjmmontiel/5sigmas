# Reasoning Models

## Reasoning is a process.

A complex problem can be broken into steps. Those steps consume time and compute. And they can fail.



## The budget is also a choice.

At inference time, you can spend more steps, samples, verification or tool interactions. On some tasks that can improve the answer, but it also increases cost and latency.

Snell et al., 2024 · optimal test-time compute

## One series, five questions.

First: what reasoning means for an LLM. Second: how these systems fail. Third: how to use more inference-time compute. Fourth: what happens to latency and streaming. Fifth: when more reasoning introduces new risks.



## Thinking longer is not free.

More reasoning can improve quality on suitable tasks. It also consumes time, compute and money. And it can introduce overthinking, errors or new attack surfaces.
