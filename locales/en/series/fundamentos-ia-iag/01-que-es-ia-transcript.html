Technical family and purpose are different

AI is the broad set; it includes rule-based systems and systems that learn.

Machine learning learns from data. Deep learning uses neural networks with multiple layers.

Generating content describes a purpose: deep networks can also classify, not only generate.

Counting frequencies does not prove causation

The word urgent appears in eight of ten spam messages in this example.

It also appears in one of ten legitimate messages.

These are class-conditional frequencies; a posterior also needs priors and other evidence.
