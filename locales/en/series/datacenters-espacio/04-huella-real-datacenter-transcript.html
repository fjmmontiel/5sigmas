01:08 — Higher efficiency need not mean lower total consumption.

PUE divides total facility energy by the energy used by IT equipment.

For 1 MWh of IT energy, lowering PUE from 2 to 1.2 reduces the total to 1.2 MWh.

If the IT load grows to 3 MWh, the same PUE gives 3.6 MWh in total.
