# What can be reused or recycled from a server

[From Mine to Cluster](00_presentacion_serie.md) · [Atlas](atlas.md)

When a server comes out of service, someone inspects its boards and modules. A working part can go into another machine or be kept as a spare. If a part fails, the team can check what it would cost to repair it.

## From a useful board to metal that can be recovered

Discarded parts enter a different supply chain. A specialist takes the equipment apart and sorts materials such as boards, housings, and cables. Some of those streams are processed to recover metals such as copper.

Think of a working board and a damaged cable. The board may still do the same job inside another server. Some of the cable's material can be recovered after sorting and processing. The first path preserves the part. The second recovers material from it.
