The silicon in a chip starts with quartz from a mine.

00:16 · Many chips on one wafer.

The wafer lets us make many chips at once.

A probe touches each chip's contacts to test it.

Cut the wafer and watch one chip come free.

00:32 · The chip connects to its memory.

The chip we just cut from the wafer does the calculations.

A stack of memory sits next to it to feed it data fast. That is HBM.

Short links join the chip to its memory. Now we test the GPU.

00:48 · The GPU goes into a server.

The GPU goes on a circuit board that connects it to the server.

The board goes inside the server. The whole computer goes in a crate.

On arrival, the server slides into a cabinet called a rack.

01:04 · GPUs work across servers.

Follow the line from the power grid to the servers.

Chips get hot as they work. Cooling carries that heat away.

Servers that share the work over a network form a cluster.

01:20 · Reuse parts, recover metals.

When a server comes out of service, its parts are checked.

A working board can move into another server.

A recycler can recover metals from discarded parts.
