# Why each chip layer needs to be clean

[From Mine to Cluster](00_presentacion_serie.md) · [Atlas](atlas.md)

Before adding another layer to a chip, the factory needs a clean wafer surface. A particle can ruin a circuit, so the factory rinses the surface with water from which almost all impurities have been removed. This is called **ultrapure water**.

## What water, gases, and chemicals do

Gases can displace air in a chamber or supply material that becomes part of a layer. Other chemicals remove material from the wafer. To flatten a surface, the factory can use a liquid with abrasive particles and a polishing pad.

Water and gases also have a path out of the process. The factory collects water for treatment and uses equipment to reduce emissions from certain gases. Filters and treatment are part of what keeps the factory running.
