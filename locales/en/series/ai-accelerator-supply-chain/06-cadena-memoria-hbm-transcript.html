Transcript of the visual text and description of the diagrams.

00:00 · Space and speed

Gigabytes tell you how much fits in memory.

Gigabytes per second tell you how much data can move each second.

Check capacity and transfer rate when comparing two specs.

A container shows how much fits; two paths show how much moves over the same interval.

00:16 · Memory goes up

HBM means high-bandwidth memory. It stacks memory chips.

Metal connections pass through the silicon and link the chips.

The stack is tested and mounted next to the compute chip.

The layers stack, connect through columns, and join the compute chip.
