00:00 · Distance changes the path

Inside a GPU, short traces connect compute and memory.

Inside a server, GPUs can communicate over direct links.

Between servers, network switches direct the traffic.

A signal travels between compute and memory, between GPUs inside one server, and between servers in different racks.

00:16 · Arrival and throughput

Latency tells you how long the first data takes to arrive.

Bandwidth tells you how much moves in the same time.

Both matter when a job has to share results.

Two time lines show that the first data can arrive early even when less data arrives in the interval.
