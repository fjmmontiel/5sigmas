# Atlas of Materials, Chips, and Companies

[Back to the GPU journey](00_presentacion_serie.md)

Look up a material to see where it is used. Copper, for example, can appear in the connections inside a chip, in cables, or in the building’s power equipment. Each entry identifies that part of the chain.

When comparing chips, look at what each number measures. **Capacity** tells you how much data the memory can hold. **Bandwidth** tells you how much it can move per second. Check whether a figure applies to a chip, one accelerator, or the whole system.

In the directory, a source may describe work a company does or confirm its role in a particular product. Open the source to see which relationship is documented.
